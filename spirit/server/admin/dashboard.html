How this is counted

One entry is one attack or one ability on one card script. An entry is stubbed while its effect is still the unimplemented marker; it counts as implemented when a hand-written coroutine or a wording pattern is bound to it, and vanilla attacks with no riders count as implemented.

Reward Ladder

Players earn ladder points per Versus match (win > loss). When their season points cross a milestone, its rewards are granted automatically — tokens go to the wallet, cards and products go to the collection. The ladder is split into tiers: the in-game screen shows one tier at a time and auto-advances to the tier holding the player's next milestone. Click a milestone's number to edit its point threshold. The game renders up to 3 reward arts per milestone (cards fan out, packs show a single tile); a milestone with several rewards must include at least one card or product.

Tournaments (in-game Events tab)

Tournaments appear on the client's Events screen while entries are open. Players pay the entry fee to queue; when the queue fills to the bracket size, a live single-elimination bracket starts and pays the prize rows by final PLACE (1 = champion). The win/loss caps and leaderboard below additionally drive the (currently UI-less) async run system.

Run Rules & Entry Fee

All fees at 0 = free entry. The Events bracket charges every non-zero fee together (gems show as Coins in-client); the async system lets the player pick one.

Play Formats (deck legality)

Which card sets are legal in each format. Decks are validated against this table when they are saved and when the client asks at login. Backed by spirit/database/json_data/formats.json (hand-editable; Save hot-reloads the server). Unlimited allows every set; basic energy is always legal everywhere.

Card Exceptions

Per-format overrides beating the set rule. Accepts archetype GUIDs or SET/number refs (e.g. SWSH8/215), separated by commas or new lines. Find GUIDs/numbers via the card search in the Codes reward picker.
